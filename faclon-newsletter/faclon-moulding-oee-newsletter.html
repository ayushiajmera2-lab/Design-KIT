The hidden problem

Losses That Never Reach the Shift Report

Moulding losses don't come from one breakdown. They build cycle after cycle.

Machines keep cycling, so everything looks fine. Meanwhile, downtime can be underreported, cycle times drift, moulds wear over time and defects reduce good-part output.

OEE PillarWhere Output Is Lost
AvailabilityUnplanned downtime, long mould changeovers, unlogged stoppages
PerformanceMicrostops, cycle-time drift, blocked or lost cavities
QualityShort shots, flash, sink marks and warpage from worn tooling

These losses can increase when moulds run on the wrong press, and this is often difficult to trace back to the mould–press pairing.

The solution

Faclon Labs OEE for Injection Moulding

Faclon Labs connects machines, moulds, materials and operators into one live view of production performance across presses, shifts and plants.

Every loss is captured, classified and linked to the machine, mould, job or shift where it occurs.

  2. CaptureCapture downtime, changeovers, cycles and rejections automatically.
  3. AnalyseAnalyse losses by press, mould, job, operator and shift.
  4. RecommendRecommend the right mould–press pairing and maintenance timing.
  5. ImproveImprove OEE continuously against each mould's golden cycle.

A single view to understand where OEE is being lost and where it can be improved.
