01The Problem

Small Conveyor Issues Can Become Big Production Problems

Conveyors run long, fast and often enclosed. Manual rounds can't keep pace.

Belt misalignment, slippage and tears develop between inspections

Tray jams and overlaps disrupt flow during high-speed operation

Shuttle proximity sensors fail under dust and material buildup, causing wrong dispensing and overflows

Pipe conveyor belts stay folded, so seam separation and tears go unseen until spillage occurs

The result: unplanned stoppages, material loss and reactive maintenance.
02The Solution

Continuous Visibility Across Every Conveyor

AI-powered cameras that watch every metre of your conveyor, every minute of every shift.

Faclon Labs combines industrial cameras with Vision AI to continuously monitor conveyor conditions, detect anomalies in real time and help maintenance teams act before faults escalate.
