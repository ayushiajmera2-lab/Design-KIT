The Challenge

Your Sootblowers Are Cleaning. But Are They Cleaning When Needed?

Sootblowing is essential for removing ash and soot deposits from boiler heat-transfer surfaces. But there is a catch:

Too little cleaning → deposits build up and heat transfer suffers.
Too much cleaning → steam is wasted and tubes can experience unnecessary erosion.

In many plants, sootblowing still relies on:

  • icon
    Fixed time intervalsBlow every X hours
  • icon
    Predefined sequencesFollow the same sequence regardless of current conditions
  • icon
    Manual interventionOperators decide when additional cleaning is required
  • icon
    Static logicLimited ability to adapt to changing boiler conditions
The problem isn't whether the boiler is being cleaned. It's whether it is being cleaned at the right time, in the right place, and for the right reason.
